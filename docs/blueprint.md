# **App Name**: AcciAssist

## Core Features:

- Accident Data Input: Quickly capture accident details, including location, date, and time, using a simplified form.
- Intelligent Advice: Based on accident details, offer tailored first aid and safety advice with a generative AI tool. The AI will consider information such as potential injuries and the surrounding environment to generate helpful responses.
- Evidence Collection: Guide users to collect critical evidence by using the phone's camera to collect photos, video, and audio records of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and witness statements.
- Emergency Notifications: Immediately alert pre-selected emergency contacts with accident details and location.
- Emergency Services Access: Provide immediate access to local emergency services and helplines.
- Emergency Dispatch: With a single click, send the accident location to the nearest hospital and ambulance services for immediate dispatch.
- Ambulance Patient Dashboard: Provide a dashboard within the ambulance to input patient details, injuries, and health conditions en route to the hospital.
- Real-time Data Sharing: Share real-time patient data from the ambulance to the hospital, including injuries, health conditions, and vital signs, enabling doctors to prepare in advance.
- Hospital Management Dashboard: Offer a hospital dashboard to view incoming patient details, manage patient beds, and arrange necessary resources like doctors and equipment.
- User-Specific Dashboards: Develop separate dashboards for hospital staff, patients, and doctors to manage information and access relevant features.
- Inventory Management: Implement inventory management features to track medical supplies and equipment within the hospital.
- Ambulance Live Location: Display the live location of ambulances on a map to facilitate coordination and estimated arrival times.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A calming blue (#5DADE2), instilling trust and reliability during stressful situations.
- Background color: Light blue (#EBF4FA), provides a gentle backdrop to ensure clarity without straining the eyes.
- Accent color: A reassuring teal (#45B39D), will highlight essential CTAs like emergency contact buttons or immediate action prompts.
- Use clear and legible sans-serif fonts to ensure information is quickly and easily understood in stressful situations.
- Use universally recognized icons to facilitate quick comprehension, particularly for emergency functions.
- Emphasize clarity and ease of navigation with a clean, intuitive layout, ensuring critical functions are easily accessible even under stress.
- Utilize subtle transitions and feedback animations to acknowledge user actions and provide assurance without causing additional stress or distraction.
